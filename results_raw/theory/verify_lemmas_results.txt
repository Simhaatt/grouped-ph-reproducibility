  E10e answer: was the profiled loss ever negative in this sweep? NO
  Over 12 configurations spanning three hazard shapes and four effect
  sizes, I*_inf - I*_T stayed strictly positive.  That is consistent
  with Lemma 3.2: profiling subtracts a credit from the loss but does
  not reverse its sign.  It is a numerical finding over this design
  space, not a proof, and the paper should say so in those words.
