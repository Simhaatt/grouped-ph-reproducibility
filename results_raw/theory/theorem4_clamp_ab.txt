READING
  The unclamped estimator -- the one Theorem 4 actually describes -- RESPECTS
  the bound at -1.006, reproducing the original 2026-08-22 run (-1.005, same
  CI) to three decimals.  The clamped estimator does not.  The gate is
  therefore pinned to clamp_inputs=False; see verify_theorem4.g_mse.

  The MSE ratio row above is the mechanism, and it is monotone:
    n=500  0.740   clamping helps 26% where the sample is thinnest
    n=2000 0.964   ~break-even
    n=16000 1.139  clamping HURTS 14% once variance is small
  Constant extrapolation is a BIAS.  It pays while variance dominates and
  costs once it does not, so the error curve flattens with n -- which is
  exactly how a log-log slope moves from -1.006 to -0.873.  Bias-variance,
  not a defect in the estimator.
