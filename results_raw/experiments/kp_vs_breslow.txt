  CONSEQUENCE FOR THE PAPER.  The formulation effect is a BASELINE
  ESTIMATOR effect.  The grouped cloglog model and Cox-plus-Breslow
  are the same model; what differs is that the grouped likelihood
  gets the profile MLE for the baseline for free while the standard
  Cox prediction pipeline substitutes a moment estimator.

  That is still a real and large finding -- the Breslow column reaches
  13.7 standard errors -- and it carries a directly usable
  recommendation: when predicting interval probabilities from a Cox
  fit on heavily tied data, use a Kalbfleisch-Prentice baseline
  rather than Breslow, or equivalently fit the grouped model.
